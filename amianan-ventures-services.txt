CATEGORY 3: CONTRIBUTOR PORTAL (/contribute)
──────────────────────────────────────────
Free platform for founders, researchers, and builders to publish content.

Content Types Accepted:
  1. Founder Story
     - Personal journey building a startup or MSME

  2. Perspective
     - Opinion piece on an ecosystem issue or trend

  3. Program Recap
     - Recap of an event, program, or initiative attended or run

  4. Ecosystem Spotlight
     - Highlight of an organization, community, or initiative

  5. Field Notes
     - Research observations or thesis findings relevant to the region

Contributor Benefits:
  - Reach thousands of readers (founders, TBI staff, investors, students, government)
  - Build credibility with a bylined piece on a recognized regional media platform
  - Grow network within the ecosystem builder community
  - Shape the innovation narrative for Northern Luzon
  - Track article impact and readership
  - Completely free — no fees, no exclusivity

How it works:
  Step 1 — Create account (email + contributor profile)
  Step 2 — Submit article (form with headline, summary, and draft or Google Docs link)
  Step 3 — Editorial review (5–7 business days)
  Step 4 — Published on amiananventures.org with byline, shared across channels
